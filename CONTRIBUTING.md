# Contributing to Oriel

Thanks for wanting to help. This repository holds Oriel's downloads, release notes and pictures; Oriel's source code isn't public. So the best ways to help are:

## Report a bug

1. Check the [open issues](https://github.com/Niix-x/Oriel-Releases/issues) first; if yours is there, add a 👍 or any detail you have.
2. Otherwise, [open a bug report](https://github.com/Niix-x/Oriel-Releases/issues/new?template=bug_report.yml). The form asks for:
   - your Oriel version (right-click the notch, **Settings > About**) and your Windows version,
   - what you did, what you expected, and what happened instead,
   - a screenshot or a short recording if it helps.

Please leave out personal details, such as your notifications or calendar events, from screenshots.

## Suggest an idea

Ideas are welcome in [Discussions › Ideas](https://github.com/Niix-x/Oriel-Releases/discussions/categories/ideas), where others can vote on them and add to them. A clear, worked-out idea can become a [feature request](https://github.com/Niix-x/Oriel-Releases/issues/new?template=feature_request.yml).

## Ask a question or share your setup

Use [Discussions](https://github.com/Niix-x/Oriel-Releases/discussions): **Q&A** for help, **Show and tell** for your themes, docks and pill layouts.

## Pull requests

Pull requests here are welcome for the README, the release notes and typos. For anything in Oriel itself, please open an issue or a discussion instead.

## Be kind

Everyone taking part follows the [code of conduct](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md).
