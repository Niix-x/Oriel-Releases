# Getting help with Oriel

- **How do I…?** Ask in [Discussions › Q&A](https://github.com/Niix-x/Oriel-Releases/discussions/categories/q-a). Someone may already have asked, so search first.
- **Something's broken.** [Open a bug report](https://github.com/Niix-x/Oriel-Releases/issues/new?template=bug_report.yml) with your Oriel and Windows versions.
- **I have an idea.** Share it in [Discussions › Ideas](https://github.com/Niix-x/Oriel-Releases/discussions/categories/ideas).
- **I found a security problem.** Please don't post it publicly; see [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

## Common answers

- **Windows says "Windows protected your PC".** The setup isn't signed yet. Click **More info**, then **Run anyway**.
- **Where are my settings?** In `%APPDATA%\Oriel`. They stay when you update or reinstall.
- **How do I open Settings?** Right-click the notch, or Oriel's icon in the tray.
- **How do I uninstall?** **Settings > Apps > Installed apps** in Windows.
