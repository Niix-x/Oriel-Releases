# Oriel privacy policy

*Last updated: 17 November 2026*

Oriel (also listed as "Oriel Notch" in the Microsoft Store) is a notch for the top of your Windows screen. It is made by Niixx_andria. This page explains what Oriel does with your information.

## The short version

Oriel keeps your data on your PC. It has no account, no analytics, no ads and no tracking, and it doesn't send your data to its maker.

## What stays on your PC

- **Settings, tasks, water and focus history, the shelf and the dock's layout**: plain-text files in Oriel's data folder (`%APPDATA%\Oriel`, or the app's own data folder when installed from the Microsoft Store).
- **Notifications**: if you allow it, Oriel reads your Windows notifications to show them in the notch. They are shown and kept on your PC only, and are not sent anywhere.
- **Calendar**: if you allow it, Oriel reads your Windows calendars to show your agenda. Events stay on your PC.
- **Calendar links and the Spotify sign-in**: stored in Windows Credential Manager.
- **What's playing**: read from Windows' media controls to show in the notch.

## What goes online, and why

Oriel connects to the internet only for these features:

| Feature | Sent to | What is sent |
|---|---|---|
| Lyrics | LRCLIB (lrclib.net) and lrcmux.dev | The title, artist and length of the song playing |
| Weather | wttr.in | Your approximate location (from your IP address, or the place you set) |
| Public holidays | Nager.Date (date.nager.at) | Your country |
| Calendar links | The address you add | A normal request for that calendar |
| Spotify (if you connect it) | Spotify | What Spotify needs to show and control your music |
| Updates (GitHub version only) | GitHub (api.github.com) | A request for the latest version |

These services have their own privacy policies. Oriel sends them nothing else.

## Children

Oriel is not aimed at children and collects no personal information from anyone.

## Removing your data

Uninstall Oriel from **Settings > Apps > Installed apps**. The GitHub version's uninstaller offers to delete your data, and the Microsoft Store version's data is removed with the app.
