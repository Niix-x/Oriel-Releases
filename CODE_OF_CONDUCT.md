# Code of conduct

Oriel's community follows the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/), version 2.1. This page sums it up; the full text at that link applies.

## Our pledge

We want taking part in Oriel's community to be a harassment-free experience for everyone, whatever their age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, colour, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that help keep this an open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive and healthy community.

## Our standards

Things that make the community a good place:

- Being kind and empathetic toward other people
- Respecting opinions, viewpoints and experiences that differ from yours
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback
- Owning up to mistakes, apologising to those affected, and learning from them
- Focusing on what is best for the whole community, not just for ourselves

Things that are not acceptable:

- Sexualised language or imagery, and sexual attention or advances of any kind
- Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks
- Public or private harassment
- Publishing other people's private information, such as a physical or email address, without their explicit permission
- Any other conduct that could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting

## Where this applies

Everywhere in Oriel's community spaces: issues, discussions, pull requests and comments in this repository, and anywhere someone is officially representing the project.

## Reporting

If something here makes you uncomfortable, tell the maintainer, [@Niix-x](https://github.com/Niix-x), by sending a private message through GitHub, or use **Report content** on the comment or post in question. Every report is reviewed promptly and fairly, and the privacy of the person reporting is respected.

## Enforcement

The maintainer may remove, edit or reject comments, issues, discussions and other contributions that break this code of conduct, and depending on how serious the behaviour is, may respond with:

1. **A correction**: a private, written note explaining what was wrong. A public apology may be asked for.
2. **A warning**: with consequences for continued behaviour, such as a period without interacting with the people involved.
3. **A temporary ban** from interacting with the community.
4. **A permanent ban** from the community, for a pattern of violations or for harassing an individual.
